The hero "Atividade diária" graphic. Rings sweep once on mount.

```jsx
<ActivityRings size={150} rings={[
  {value:11000,max:16000,label:'Passos'},
  {value:440,max:680,label:'Calorias'},
  {value:1.8,max:2.5,color:'var(--water-500)',label:'Água'}
```

Always accompanied by the numeric legend — the rings alone never carry the value.
